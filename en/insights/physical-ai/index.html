What Is Physical AI?

When you ask ChatGPT to "make me coffee," it explains the process beautifully. But it can't actually brew a cup — because ChatGPT exists only in the digital world.

Physical AI breaks through this limitation. AI that sees with eyes (cameras, sensors), thinks with a brain (AI models), and acts with hands and feet (robot arms, wheels). That's Physical AI.

TypeWhat It DoesExamples
Traditional AI (ChatGPT, etc.)Generates digital outputsWriting, drawing, coding

One-Sentence Definition

Physical AI is an intelligent system that perceives the environment through sensors, makes decisions with AI, and takes direct action in the physical world through actuators.

① Data — Gathering Experience

Collecting real data (factory cameras, sensors, LiDAR) and synthetic data (AI-generated in virtual environments). NVIDIA's Cosmos World Foundation Model auto-generates thousands of scenario variations from small amounts of real footage.

🧠

② Training — Learning Skills

Training VLA models through imitation learning (learning from human demonstrations) and reinforcement learning (finding optimal methods through trial and error). VLA integrates Vision + Language + Action.

🎮

③ Simulation — Practicing in Virtual Worlds

Extensive practice in digital twins (virtual replicas of reality). Like pilots training hundreds of hours in flight simulators before actual flights.

🌉

④ Sim-to-Real — Bridging the Gap

Reducing the subtle differences between virtual and real worlds (Sim-to-Real Gap) through Domain Randomization, then deploying optimized models to robot edge computers.

🤖

⑤ Agentic Orchestration — Autonomous Operation

Multiple AI agents collaborate for task planning, anomaly detection, human-robot cooperation, and self-improvement to autonomously handle complex operations.

The Brain of Physical AI: VLM and VLA

VLM vs VLA — The Core Difference

VLM (Vision-Language Model) — "AI that sees and understands." The commentator analyzing the game. Sees images, responds with text.

VLA (Vision-Language-Action Model) — "AI that sees, understands, and acts." The player on the field. Takes images and instructions, generates robot action commands.

In real systems, VLM sees the big picture and plans (the coach), while VLA executes the actions (the player).

The Big Picture

Physical AI isn't just "smarter robots." It's the beginning of a new era where AI crosses the boundary of the digital world to directly see, decide, and act in the physical world we live in.

10 million factories, 200,000 warehouses, 1.5 billion vehicles worldwide — Physical AI can be applied to all of them. CES 2026 featured a record 38 humanoid robot companies.
